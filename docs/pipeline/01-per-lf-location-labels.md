## Per-LF location & labels

- **`derive_per_lf_locations` is total**: every LF needs centroid-derivable GPS or an operator `--per-lf-location 'INDEX=LABEL'` override. There is no drive-wide `--location` fallback (it silently produced identical OST across multi-LF days). PhaseBC entry gate refuses encoding when any LF lacks both; OST-burn has a defense-in-depth assertion.
- **Operator override chooses the TEXT for its LF — burned verbatim; every gate still checks it.** The location-invariant coverage gate and the specificity ladder validate an override like any other label (no bypass; a typo that omits a material county fails pre-encode). A non-blank `--per-lf-location` short-circuits both `per_lf_ost_text` derivation sites (after `validate_burned_ost_text`), bypassing the GPS cascade that would reshape it. Argparse rejects an override equal to `--location`. It labels the LF and that LF's shorts on a GPS-less day only: a GPS-bearing short burns its own clip-GPS place or hard-fails (`59-shorts.md`), because the LF label names the whole drive, never one 30-second clip of it. On a day with GPS, a clip with no fix of its own is never planned as a short.
- **Location claims fail closed.** The specificity ladder fails, never skips, a label with no evidence record, a blank label, or a record with neither a GPS trace nor a voted county/state; the pre-encode gate requires an evidence record for every LF that has a `ResolvedLocation`. An unresolved geocoder county (an independent city such as Bristol, VA) casts no region vote — never a `"None, Virginia"` region (`gps_enrichment.region_label`). The description's "Started in X, ended in Y" comes from the burned label's own "A to B" endpoints (`content_gates.title_endpoints`), never the first/last geocoded town; a label without endpoints makes no endpoint claim. A short's state tag reads only the label's trailing state slot, so "Washington County, Virginia" tags VA, not WA.
- **Resume re-applies the override everywhere** (`drive_orchestrator.preserve_per_lf_overrides_on_resume`): a non-empty `--per-lf-location` on resume wins over saved `per_lf_overrides` AND rewrites each already-populated Phase-A-derived map (`per_lf_locations` / `per_lf_ost_text` / `per_lf_titles`) for its index. A `--phase D` resume never re-derives those maps, so without this the saved label still became the title (a saved label with a leading preposition otherwise hard-failed the metadata gate despite a corrected `1=Ballyford, County Cork` override). On a finals-only resume the burned pixels keep the old OST; title/thumbnail/metadata take the new label.
- **Operator title word vs. place noun** (`_series_utils.parse_lf_label`): an override like `"To Ballyford, County Cork"` is an OST-burn TITLE, not a place noun. `parse_lf_label` returns `LfLabel(display, place, region)` — `display` is the label verbatim (kept for the OST burn / video title, via `title_override`/`per_lf_ost_text`, untouched by this change); `place` strips a leading curated title word (`To`/`Through`/`Up around`/`Around`/`The road to`/`Toward(s)`, case-insensitive) so a plain label is a no-op; `region` is the tail after `place`'s last comma. Every other noun slot reads `place`: `longform_metadata._build_gms_description` reassigns its local `location` to `place` at entry (hook, SEO search line, factual body, tags, geo hashtags, `📍 Location:` all inherit it), `longform_metadata._location_tags` parses internally, and `social_generator._arc_safe_anchor` (already the single reducer for `location_short`/`social_location`/hashtags) parses after its existing arc/via reduction. The video title/thumbnail path (`_build_title` off `title_override`/`location`) is untouched — it still burns `display`.
- **GPS labels vote the dominant county, never the bare centroid.** Per-LF base, "X to Y" title, and shorts OST reduce via `title_builder.dominant_location` — the modal `(county,state)` voted across sampled, quality-filtered points, footage-span-weighted (`_evenly_sample_weighted`), collapsed county-first BEFORE the tally: a stateless variant folds onto its county's single non-empty state, but two distinct non-empty states under one county name (Washington County TN/VA) are different counties and are never merged, their stateless samples are dropped, and an exact top-weight tie across two states returns None (one real county can reverse-geocode under two "state" variants, e.g. an Irish county tagged once with its province-as-state and once stateless; keying the vote on the bare `(county,state)` tuple splits that one county across two buckets so a true-minority county steals the plurality; the collapse folds each county's samples onto one key, and a normal single-state county has one state so the tally is byte-identical), then selected by `min(votes, key=(-weight,county,state))` (deterministic, direction-independent; alphabetical last-resort tie-break). A centroid lands on the wrong side of a county line. Geocode quorum floor `GEOCODE_QUORUM_MIN=2`: below 2 geocoded samples the plurality is too weak → `dominant_location` returns None. In `derive_per_lf_locations` there is **no bare-centroid geocode fallback** (deleted — it stamped the wrong-side county): a quorum-degraded or vote-less LF joins the aggregated ambiguous-input `ValueError` and the operator supplies `--per-lf-location 'INDEX=LABEL'`. The shorts OST closure keeps its degrade-to-no-label behavior (OST derivation never fails a short) but the degrade is LOUD: quorum-miss and exception both WARN with the short's rank. Parallel road ties route through `_dominant_by_distance`.
- **Via-road quality filter** (`title_builder._road_passes_quality`): a via-road surfaces ONLY as a named through-route — route ref (`US-64`/`N86`, `_ROUTE_REF_RE`), through-road name suffix (Parkway/Highway/…, `_THROUGH_ROAD_NAME_RE`), known byway, or an OSM through-road highway class. Residential/track/service/local/business names are rejected (clause omitted). Far-road drop: endpoints `> _VIA_ROAD_MAX_SPAN_KM` (50 km) apart set `road=None` in every shape (a local road can't describe the crossing) — but a route-ref road (`_ROUTE_REF_RE`: interstate/US/state highway) is EXEMPT, since a numbered through-route honestly connects distant endpoints (a drive can stay on one interstate for 100 km). The multi-road (`_build_multi_road_title`) and non-contiguous drops are not exempted. The unfiltered `per_lf_location` suffix road also passes through `_road_passes_quality`.
- **Via-road modal vote + class-aware tie policy** (`road_votes` in `dominant_location`): the via-road is the modal road across the winning county's quality-passing samples, with every member of an OSM concurrency `ref` credited (`_road_ref_members` — "I 77;I 81;US 52" credits all three). Ties are resolved by vote and road class, never signature or alphabet: a single top-voted member wins; on a tie, a lone interstate (`_INTERSTATE_REF_RE`, `^I-\d+$`) beats its companions ("I 81;US 11" → I-81); a tie holding two-plus interstates — or none — emits NO via-road. The prior signature-based pure-concurrency guard missed windows spanning two overlap signatures with the same interstates (US-11 joining the I-77/I-81/US-52 overlap mid-window), letting the alphabetical tie-break stamp "via I-77" on an I-81 drive.
- **Recognizable-place ladder** (`build_lf_title`): a title anchors on a recognizable settlement from `ResolvedLocation.ordered_place_sequence` before collapsing to the bare county. `_recognizable_settlements` drops `… County` entries and possessive business names; `_looks_like_county` nulls a county-shaped endpoint town so the ladder fires. A resolved endpoint town wins over the sequence. `_geocode_town` prefers the most significant settlement class — `city`/`town` before `village`/`hamlet` — and never reads the POI-prone `name`, so a rural endpoint resolves to a real settlement instead of a name the county guard would demote. **Rank-aware winning-county name** (`_pick_ranked_name`): `DominantLocation.name` is picked across ALL winning-county samples by settlement class (city/town > village > hamlet > locality/suburb > bare `name`, refined by Nominatim `addresstype`/`place_rank` when the gps service supplies them; absent = neutral), then accumulated footage weight, then travel order — so a first-sampled hamlet ("Hillcrest") never outranks the county's real town ("Exampleton"), and the Level-4 anchor adopts this pick when it is one of the sequence's recognizable settlements. An LF with no settlement keeps the county anchor or the operator override. **Stateless-county tail**: `build_lf_title`'s `region_label` gate — which every settlement/via-road/A-to-B branch requires truthy — is derived from the label's tail after its first comma. A stateless county (Irish counties, US independent cities) with no `state` produced a bare, comma-less label ("County Cork"), so `region_label` came back empty and the WHOLE cascade was skipped regardless of an available settlement/road — collapsing straight to the Level-5 raw-label fallback ("County Cork" verbatim). `derive_per_lf_locations` now appends the geocoded `country` (already carried on the vote) as the tail when `state` is absent ("County Cork, Ireland"), unlocking the ladder; a stateless county with no `country` signal either keeps the old bare label.
- **Shared shape ladder**: both title surfaces (`ost_formatter._format_ost_from_route` and `RouteProfile.title`) delegate to the single `ost_formatter.format_location_ladder(...)` — the only place shape priority lives (cross-state → two-town → feature → one-town+road → road/county ↔ cross-county → road/state). Two policy flags (`two_town_state_suffix`, `cross_county_before_road`) capture the historical surface differences without duplicating the ladder. Same-endpoint labels never emit `"X to X"`. `dominant_road` requires a true `>50%` distance plurality, and the slot is quality-gated by `route_profile._road_slot_quality_ok`: RouteAnalyzer segments carry no OSM `place_class`, so a name clearing `_road_passes_quality` (route-ref / curated byway / through-suffix) keeps the bare `>50%` bar, while an UNCLASSED name ("Avenue Example") also needs `UNCLASSED_ROAD_MIN_SHARE` (≥75%) of the route AND `UNCLASSED_ROAD_MIN_KM` (≥5 km) driven — a failed gate emits NO road (never promotes the runner-up). A hard name-only filter was rejected (Shape-5 named-road contract: a legitimately named scenic road with real dominance stays eligible); carrying `place_class` on gps-side segments is the future class-aware upgrade.
- **Cross-boundary demotion is voted, county AND state** (`_format_ost_from_route`): before the ladder, a cross-county OR cross-state endpoint claim is corroborated against the central-window midpoint, VOTED across several samples (`_vote_mid_region` in `pipeline/place_shape.py`, engine-side; `title_builder` re-exports it) so one flaky mid geocode can't fail the demotion open. When the voted mid county matches the start county the cross-county span demotes to single-county; when the voted mid **state** matches the start state a single flaky end-waypoint geocode flaked across a state line, so the cross-state span demotes to the single-state shape (never a bogus "…, SC to …, NC" on an SC-only drive). A genuine crossing (mid in a third region, or inconclusive) keeps both endpoints. An exact top tie in the mid vote (e.g. 2-2 across a real crossing) is inconclusive too and keeps both endpoints; it is never broken alphabetically.
- **Per-LF route-profile enrichment** (`route_profile.build_lf_profile`, wired in `phase_d_metadata._build_route_profiles_per_lf`): the metadata loop aggregates the persisted per-EPISODE `state.episode_profiles` into one per-LF profile (journey arc / merged towns / roads → description body + tags via `longform_metadata._enrich_with_route_profile`), keyed by `lf.index` and persisted to `state.route_profiles`. Episodes map to their LF by recording DATE (`per_lf_recording_date` × `episode_dates`) — never the DAY-LOCAL segment `source_file_index`, which after `_partition_lf_plans_by_date` aliases another day's episodes. Its count-based road merge applies the same `>50%` plurality guard as `build_route_profile`, so a barely-driven road never ships as `via {Road}`; because the merged weights are appearance counts (no distances), an unclassed name additionally must be some episode's already-gated `dominant_road` — a junk name arriving via `roads_traversed` alone (trivially 100% of a one-road list) is dropped. GPS-less LFs get no entry (consumer sees `None` → no enrichment). On a multi-LF day the day-wide aggregate is then re-scoped per LF: journey arc + towns from the LF's own `ResolvedLocation.ordered_place_sequence` (`_rescope_arc_to_lf`), and the `roads_traversed` list (road tags + the "Roads traveled:" description line) from the LF's own episode span via `per_lf_gpx_episode_paths` re-aggregated through the same `build_lf_profile` merge (`_rescope_roads_to_lf` — the per-episode `ROADS_TRAVERSED_MIN_KM` floor is inherited, not re-derived). Single-LF days and legacy state without the span map keep the day aggregate. **`_enrich_with_route_profile` gates every road through `title_builder._road_passes_quality`** — the same gate the title's via-road slot uses — before any road reaches the description's "Roads traveled:" / "Driving through … on …" line or the tag loop; the older `route_profile._road_slot_quality_ok` share/distance floor alone let a briefly-crossed town street ("Mill Street") or a generic named lane ("The Mill Lane") through. A ref-numbered (`N71`) or named through-road (`Example Parkway`) still ships; a street/lane/town road never does. If every road in the list fails the gate, the road clause is omitted entirely rather than emitting an empty `on .` / `Roads traveled: .`. **The journey-arc sentence names each intermediate town once, in first-visit order** (`towns_traversed` minus the endpoints, case-insensitive): a route that loops back through a town — `… passing through Exampleton Township, Exampleton, Exampleton` — keeps the true sequence on the `🛤️ Route:` line only; the prose never repeats a name (`tests/test_description_richness.py::test_journey_arc_names_a_revisited_town_once`).
- **Non-US trailing region** is used verbatim: `region_label = state_abbr or state_part` backs the trailing `", {region}"` (e.g. `"Killarney to Dingle via N86, Kerry"`).
- **`--label-dry-run`** / **`--list-lfs`** run Phase A only and print resolved labels + county-vote breakdown, plus the resolved via-road and its per-member road-vote tally (so via-road behaviour is verifiable pre-encode), or the LF index table — all without encoding, the pre-encode verification gate. In `full_drive`, `--list-lfs` skips the A3 scoring pass so segments carry no `gps_center`; an LF with no segment centroid is labelled from the GPX fixes covering its span via the same `_build_gps_points_by_lf` + `derive_per_lf_locations` path the encode uses (never a second deriver). `<no GPS — override required>` prints only when that span truly has no usable GPS county vote. `--label-dry-run` also prints each LF's title — `assemble_final_title` of the canonical OST text the render burns (both the dry-run and `_enrich_per_lf_gps` call `gps_enrichment.canonical_lf_ost_text` per LF, then the sibling-differentiation pass; operator override verbatim; the county label line above it is not the title source; the cross-LF `unique_titles` suffix is not reproduced) — and a greppable `TITLE-COLLISION` line (live video id when the snapshot knows it) when that title is already on the channel per the on-disk ledger or `<storage_path>/live_catalog.latest.json` — read-only, no network, so a title published after the last snapshot can still slip past it; the Phase BC exact-title-collision gate, which refreshes the channel, stays the hard refusal. Neither flag ever runs the master-chain calibration encode: pre-flight only loads a saved calibration record (as `--dry-run` does), so a full_drive label check, which takes no GPU lock, never puts NVENC load on a GPU another render holds. When the run requests insert photos (`--insert-photos`), `--label-dry-run` also lists each photo with its EXIF capture county and the LFs whose dry-run label accepts it (`county_in_label`, the insert gate's county test), or `no EXIF GPS` / `county unresolved` / `outside every LF label`; the verdict uses the labels printed above, not `--per-lf-location` overrides.
- **Deterministic sibling-LF differentiation** (`title_builder.differentiate_sibling_labels` + `ranked_lf_features`): when ≥2 LFs across any day collapse to the same label (rural no-settlement class), each is differentiated by its modal named feature (footage-weighted, dominant-feature-only). Renders `"{region} — {feature}, {ST}"`. Where geography genuinely overlaps (two LFs share the dominant road), the LF is left on the clean region base — YouTube titles get `(Part N)` via `ensure_unique_titles`, the burned OST stays the clean region. No per-run human/LLM judgment. 
- **A leading bare preposition is refused at launch** (`cli.parser._validate_per_lf_location_args`, rule 0): `--per-lf-location '1=To Ballyford, County Cork'` exits 2 before any encode, using the same `_GRAMMAR_LEADING_PREP_RE` the pre-upload metadata gate applies. The YouTube title derives from the burned label, so such a label always hard-failed at publish hours later. The title-word parsing above still governs GPS/derived labels; an operator passes the place name.
- **Split `full_drive` parts (`RegularLFPlan.part_index`/`part_count`, set only when a day splits)**: every per-part number, location and chapter window is keyed by `lf.index` from that part's own segments. A part with no windowed source of its own (no `per_lf_gpx_episode_paths` entry and no kept ranges) drops `distance`/`speed` rather than shipping the whole-trace numbers or the `Full drive:` flag (`phase_d_metadata._build_metadata_per_lf`). Chapters are generated per part from that part's ranges, so offsets restart at 0. The metadata title gets the superlong `— Part k of K` suffix (`title_builder.append_full_drive_part_suffix`, idempotent, clamped to 100 chars; burned OST never carries it); part LFs are excluded from the incidental `(Part N of M)` dedup so the two suffixes never stack. `part_count` of `None`/1 changes nothing.
- **A cross-county endpoint with material footage is never a flake; Level 2b names both counties** (`title_builder._build_lf_title_cascade`): `_validate_route_endpoints(..., county_shares=)` receives each county's share of the footage-weighted `dominant_location` vote (`_county_shares`). An endpoint whose `(county, state)` share is at least `LOCATION_MATERIAL_SHARE` (0.20, a policy constant beside the location-invariant floors in `pipeline/validation/content_gates.py`, not a measurement) is kept even when the midpoint matches the other county — a 56/44 drive's midpoint sits in the larger county, so the midpoint alone used to erase the real second location (a drive from Alphatown to Betaville labelled with only the larger county). Only an endpoint below the floor (a true flake has ~zero footage) is demoted; `county_shares=None` (no GPS trace) keeps midpoint-only behaviour. When two counties are material but an endpoint town is still unresolved after the settlement backfills, Level 2b (before the Level-4 county fallback) names both: `"{A} & {B} County, {ST}"` (`"{A} County, S1 & {B} County, S2"` across states, `"County A & County B"` for non-US labels), in start-to-end order when both endpoint counties are material, else by share (`_material_counties`, `_multi_county_label`).
- **The location gates FAIL, never warn, when a label omits a county with material footage** (`content_gates.check_location_invariant`; a label must never name one location and show another). Every county holding at least `LOCATION_MATERIAL_SHARE` of the vote must be NAMED by the shipped claim: its county name appears in the title / OST location / description 📍 or SEO line / hashtag / tag, or an `"A to B"` title endpoint town maps to it through `ResolvedLocation.endpoint_regions` (`{"start": "County, State", "end": ...}`, the reverse-geocoded county of the first / last trace point, set in `build_resolved_location`, absent for a withheld or unresolved endpoint, carried to both gate call sites by `resolved_location_gate_dict`; first title phrase = start, last = end, so two towns in one county name only that county). A legacy model without `endpoint_regions` can only satisfy the rule by naming the county. Failure message: `label ... omits {County, State} which holds {share} of the footage`. A unanimous vote (one material county) behaves as before; a county below the floor needs no mention. The same gate runs pre-encode (Phase BC `_assert_pre_encode_location_invariant`, raises before any encode) and post-encode (Phase D). The old WARN-only plurality check (`LOCATION-INVARIANT-AMBIGUOUS`) is gone. **Shorts have no floor:** `check_short_location_invariant(sampled_vote=...)` hard-fails when the clip's own GPS vote spans two or more counties at any share (a short is single-county by construction; `short_regions_fn` returns the regions with a `.vote` attribute), still upstream of the 9:16 encode.
- **A label claims only what the RETAINED footage's own GPS proves (`pipeline/route_claims.py`).** After planning and before the burn, `_enrich_per_lf_gps` builds one `RouteWindow` per kept LF segment (`_build_route_windows_by_lf`: the segment's own fixes in segment media seconds, no dedup), and `enforce_route_claims` measures each LF. Excluded footage supplies no claim. One fix per 30 s bin is reverse-geocoded; a place with at least `MIN_PLACE_S` (60 s) of retained time is required and the label must name it, so a route that leaves and returns to its start still names the place in between. Every place the label's leading clause names ("A to B via R", "A & B", "A Loop") must be one the retained fixes resolve to; naming a place with no retained fix is an over-claim. Each segment reads its own episode's GPX (`source_file_index`), so a day split across two GPX files feeds both. **Gap rule.** A valid fix is finite, in range, not `(0,0)`, and not a repeat of an earlier timestamp (duplicates keep the first; trackpoints carry no DOP or fix type to filter on). Fix times are the GPS trackpoint timestamp minus the episode's effective offset, in episode media seconds (`TIMESTAMP_SOURCE`), on the `state.gps_per_episode` timeline (`TIMELINE`, recorded as `timeline`): the phase-A parse of each episode's `.gpx`, not a fresh raw-GPX read. A verifier re-measuring from raw GPX can see sub-second per-fix shifts and a different gap count, so it re-measures on this timeline; the gap rule is the same on both. A gap is a fix-to-fix interval longer than `GAP_S`: exactly 5.0 s is not a gap, 5.01 s is. The interval runs from the nearest valid fix on each side, even one outside the window (the window carries its file's `prev_fix_s` / `next_fix_s`), so a dropout a cut falls inside still counts; a window edge with no fix beyond it in the file bounds the interval, so a leading or trailing no-fix span counts. Only the retained overlap counts toward `gap_seconds`; time between episodes never does. Nothing is interpolated and a missing endpoint is a gap, not an invented place. `coverage_pct` = 100 × (1 − `gap_seconds` ÷ retained seconds), e.g. 1000 s with a 50 s gap is 95.00 %. `unsettled_seconds` is retained time with a fix but no settlement; the refusal prints both. An interval within one tick (the median fix spacing) of 5.0 s is recorded as a `boundary_note`, never a silent call. `RouteEvidence.record()` is the sampled-evidence record: `timeline`, `timestamp_source`, `tick_s`, `gap_threshold_s`, `coverage_pct`, `gap_seconds`, `unsettled_seconds`, `gaps[]` (segment, file, media start, length, the two bounding fixes), `invalid_fixes`, `duplicate_fixes`, `samples`, places, and `containment_proven: false` with `containment_basis: "sampled_reverse_geocode"` — sampled bins never prove containment, so no label may say "verified in" or "contained" (`containment_wording_issues`). A label that over-claims falls back automatically to the narrowest region (county, then state) that every retained bin resolves to, with no gap and no sample outside (`samples_outside > 0` blocks even at 100 % coverage); otherwise the run stops with one `RouteClaimRefusedError` that names every refused LF, its places with seconds, the coverage, `gap_seconds`, `unsettled_seconds`, each gap as `<segment> <file> @<start> s +<length> s`, and the fallbacks tried. An operator `--per-lf-location` is checked the same way but never replaced. A missing geocoder with GPS present refuses. `--label-dry-run` writes `<stages_base>/route_claims_dry_run.json` (`{refused, lfs[]}`; each LF is `route_claim_outcome`: `lf_index`, `label_requested`, `label` — the kept or fallback label, `null` when refused — `refused`, `reason`, then `RouteEvidence.record()` verbatim, then `destination_source`: `overpass` when the destination POI query answered with a destination, `failed` when the POI source raised (Overpass outage; the label is built without a destination), `none` for no montage peaks or no enclosing candidate), prints its path, then prints the fallback label and any `ROUTE-CLAIM REFUSED` block; a refusal fails the run (exit 1). With no run dir known it writes nothing. Limits: a visit shorter than one bin can be missed; trackpoints carry no accuracy radius, so the evidence is sampled bins only. The LF provenance sidecar's `fix_density_pct` (the ladder's locked-GPS share of the LF duration) is a different measure from this `coverage_pct` and is not called coverage. Its `road_vote` tally counts untimed reverse-geocode samples, so each LF also records `road_coverage_measured: false` and `road_vote_basis: "reverse_geocode_votes_untimed"`: the tally is not retained-time road coverage and backs no "via <road>" claim. Montage geography and the bound review record are covered separately. Tests: `tests/test_retained_gps_claims.py`.
- **Zero-tolerance place gate (`route_claims.place_text_issues`).** A text that names a place is a claim about every retained clip. Every place a text names (settlement from `ev.places`, county with/without " County", state name/abbreviation from `ev.bins`) must hold every retained second; there is no 60 s floor for the named place. A bin is covered when its settlement, county or state is named (a settlement/county in an unnamed state is not, when the text names states); an unresolved bin or GPS gap is covered only by a named county/state whose pinned proof carries the `windows_key` of exactly these retained windows (`retained_windows_key`: segment, source, start/end to the ms) -- `pinned_boundary_proof` refuses a keyless proof, and a valid proof still fails a sample geocoded to another region. Applied in `label_issues` (LF/Short OST; the region fallback must itself pass), `_retained_title_issues` (LF title) and `final_text_record.check_final_text` (title, description, tags-as-one-text, before the insert). Offline re-check of a finished render: `route_claims.recheck_place_text(per_lf_route_record[idx]["record"], text)`; `RouteEvidence.record()` now carries `windows_key` and per-place `bins`, and a record without `bins` is refused as unverifiable.
- **Short title, description and tags (same gate).** A Short's route record (`make_short_route_fn(...).last_record`, i.e. `RouteEvidence.record()` with `bins` and `windows_key`) is persisted per rank in `shorts_ost_audit_<date>.json` (`route_record`, excluded from the audit hash), carried by `phase_d_scheduling` -> `SocialPost.route_record` -> `PublishRecord.route_record` in the plan sidecar. `short_final_text_context` hands it to `check_final_text`, so before the insert the Short's title, description and tags (as one text) must each hold every retained second of the clip. A GPS-bearing Short with no persisted record or no `bins` (planned before this gate) is refused as unverifiable (`place_outside_text`): re-measure/re-render it.
- **A GPS-bearing Short's burned text claims only what its own clip's GPS shows (`make_short_route_fn`).** The same rule at Short scale, run in `_render_day_shorts` after the Short's text is derived and before anything for the day is rendered: the clip's day-master GPS becomes one `RouteWindow`, binned at `GAP_S` (5.0 s) rather than 30 s, with no floor (`_SHORT_FLOOR_S = 0.0`): any place a bin resolves to must be named, and any gap over `GAP_S` or any unsettled bin refuses. An over-claiming text falls back to the county or state that contains every bin, unchanged from the long-form fallback, and the fallback is logged. Otherwise the Short is refused. All refusals for the day are collected and raised as one `RouteClaimRefusedError` (`Short route claims REFUSED (day …); nothing rendered`) that names each rank, before any Short of that day is extracted or burned. Wired at `phase_bc_day_first` (`make_short_route_fn(geocoder)`) through `run_all_days`, `run_day_pipeline` and `media_api.RunSpec`. A GPS-less Short skips the check. Tests: `tests/test_short_route_claims.py`.
- **Superlong parts, the montage and the LF thumbnail claim only what was checked (`make_superlong_route_fn`).** `_produce_superlong_parts` checks each part's burned text with the long-form rule (30 s bins, 60 s place floor, `GAP_S` gaps, region fallback) over that part's own `ranges` in day-master seconds, the same timeline as `day_gps_points`, never over the whole day. A part whose ranges show a place the text omits is broadened to the containing region or refused; a `part_transitions` lower-third naming a place no retained fix of its part resolves to is refused (`place_not_on_route`). All refusals are raised together as one `RouteClaimRefusedError` before the superlong audit, any extract or any burn. The checked text feeds the part's metadata (and so its thumbnail), the audit and `apply_ost`; each part's record is `record["route_claim"]` (`timeline`, `containment_proven: false`, gaps, coverage). A GPS day reaching `_produce_superlong_parts` with no route check refuses (fail closed). `prepend_montage` takes no text at all: the montage's only place text is the LF's checked OST, overlaid after. The LF thumbnail burns `per_lf_ost_text`, so a label `enforce_route_claims` broadened reaches it (`flatten_ost_text`). Tests: `tests/test_superlong_route_claims.py`, `tests/test_route_claim_thumbnail.py`.
- **Final-text review before every long-form insert.** Phase BC persists each LF's retained-route evidence as `state.per_lf_route_record` (place names, retained seconds, gaps and region claims; no fixes or coordinates). Phase D builds Waypost's `final-text-record-1` from it (`pipeline/final_text_record.py`) and stores the review as `metadata["final_text_review"]`. The record binds the source footage, the LF's retained segments, the day's passed OST audit hash, the exact title and the visible OST and thumbnail text. GMS's own `YouTubeUploader.upload_video` re-verifies the review just before `videos().insert` (`upload_if_needed`, `upload_video_with_playlist`), against fingerprints and text recomputed from current state. A missing or empty review, a verdict other than `agree`, or any changed text, plan, source or policy is refused (`FinalTextReviewError`) and fails the batch. A GPS-less LF has no review and stays under the existing gates. The OST confirmation gate is unchanged. Shorts are reviewed on their own path (`publishing/youtube_adapter.py`) and superlong parts through `external_systems_coordinator.superlong_final_text_inputs`.

**Viewer place names.** `route_profile.build_route_profile` (town list) and `title_builder.dominant_location` (`ordered_place_sequence`, which feeds the `🛤️ Route:` line and the per-LF arc rescope) pass each place through Waypost's `seo_manager.core.locality.viewer_localities` (see waypost `docs/viewer-locality-names.md`): a tract/subdivision/vicinity name is dropped, an island named in the place's own geocode address replaces it, else it is simply dropped (no island is inferred from coordinates: the Census St. Helena Island county subdivision also covers Lady's Island), and the dropped-name map persists as `RouteProfile.locality_substitutions`. The fallback "Driving through …" sentence names each place once, first-seen order.
- **Opt-in GPS event captions on regular long-form videos (`--gps-captions events`).** Off by default; `off` leaves `apply_ost`'s `lower_thirds` unset, as before. With `events` (`config.drive.gps_captions`, persisted as `state.gps_captions` via a `flag_registry` sentinel row, so a resume that omits the flag keeps the saved choice and the unchanged card list keeps the LF's OST fingerprint; a differing flag wins, is persisted and logged at INFO), `run_day_pipeline` first requires `state.per_lf_route_record` for every LF and raises `RouteClaimRefusedError` before the day master encodes if one is missing. Per LF it derives town events from that LF's own GPS chapters (`lf_chapters_fn`, wired in `phase_bc_day_first` to `phase_d_metadata._lf_chapters`) with the kit's `town_events`, shifts them by the montage lead (chapter times are LF-body-relative, so the shift is the probed `prepended` minus `concatenated` duration, the planned montage length only when that cannot be probed), suppresses every card naming a place no retained fix of that LF resolves to (`route_claims.lower_third_issues`, `place_not_on_route`), then spaces the rest (`regular_lf_lower_third_duration_sec` 8.0 s, `regular_lf_lower_third_min_gap_sec` 45.0 s; the superlong knobs are untouched). Cards and suppressions persist under `state.regular_lf_captions[date][lf_index]` (`lower_thirds`, `lower_thirds_suppressed`). Limit: the route evidence holds place totals, not per-second times, so a card is checked for place-on-route, not against the exact seconds it covers. Tests: `tests/test_regular_lf_gps_captions.py`.

## Route map inset on regular long-form videos (`--map-inset`)

Off by default. `--map-inset route` composites a route-only map inset (the renderer in `gomoveshift/pipeline/map_inset.py`) onto each regular long-form video, inside the same OST encode that burns the location text, so the 10-bit master is not transcoded a second time. With the flag off, the pipeline is byte-identical to before: same `apply_ost` arguments, same OST fingerprint, no state record.

Flags (all persist in drive state, so a resume that omits them keeps the first run's choice; a differing value wins):

- `--map-inset off|route` (`config.drive.map_inset`)
- `--map-inset-bundle PATH`: an operator-prepared offline bundle. The pipeline never downloads or builds one.
- `--map-inset-trim-start-m METRES` and `--map-inset-trim-end-m METRES`
- `--map-inset-privacy-zone LAT,LON,RADIUS_M` (repeatable)

The inset sits bottom-left at 20% of the frame width; there are no corner or size flags.

Refusal rules. Before any encode (before the day master is built), `route` checks its inputs and raises ONE error listing every problem and saying nothing was encoded: no bundle given, or the bundle has no `manifest.json` or fails `verify_bundle`; either trim is not greater than zero (stricter than the Route Story film CLI, which only requires one nonzero trim, because a one-sided trim still shows where a drive began or ended); an unparsable privacy zone; no usable GPS fix for the day; or photo/clip inserts on the day (they are spliced after the burn and would shift the map). Per LF, the renderer's own refusals (for example too little route coverage) stop the run, naming the LF. A day never silently falls back to no inset and never ships a partial overlay.

How it lines up. The day's GPS fixes (master seconds) are split into segments wherever two fixes are more than 5 s apart. Each LF's cut writes its keycut receipt beside the concat (`lf_NN_segments.mp4.receipt.json`); the inset timeline is that receipt shifted by the montage lead actually present in the prepended file. A concat without its receipt is cut again once. The clip is `lf_NN_map_inset.mov`; it and the receipts are deleted with the other LF work files.

Privacy. Trim and zones are applied to the route before anything is framed or drawn, and the inset shows only the recorded route and a position marker. The inset receipt (bundle checksum, trim, zones' effect, hidden-frame counts, imagery credits) is stored under `state.regular_lf_map_inset[date][lf_index]`. Changing the bundle, trim, zones or route re-burns the LF, because those are folded into the LF's OST fingerprint. Tests: `tests/test_regular_lf_map_inset.py`.
