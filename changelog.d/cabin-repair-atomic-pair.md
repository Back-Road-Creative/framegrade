### Fixed

- A cabin repair now swaps the privacy mask and its regions sidecar as a journaled pair. A pending marker beside the sidecar is written before the first replace and removed after the second; an interrupted swap leaves it. The repair, the repair loop (which refuses the pair after each repair), a pairing re-check just before the day-master encode, and the mask cache (which re-detects automatically and clears the marker) all refuse the mismatched pair; previously a crash between the two replaces left a new mask beside the old sidecar.
- A repair round in which no vehicle detector could be built is no longer counted as a spent round or recorded as an exhausted repair. The gate still fails closed, with `detector unavailable` in the error, and a retry once the model loads gets its full rounds.
