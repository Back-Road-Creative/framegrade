### Fixed

- A live-catalog video with a drive's expected title and no ledger row or receipt (a publish that died after the YouTube upload) is now an `orphan_upload` incident that refuses that drive's `health publish` with the video id, title, privacy and `publish_at`, instead of the next run uploading a second copy. The refusal says to verify which drive uploaded it, then delete it in YouTube Studio or book it into the ledger; nothing on YouTube is deleted or unlisted, other drives are untouched, the refusal counts no attempt, and an unreadable or missing ledger refuses too.
- The orphan gate knows a half-finished replace's own upload (receipts of any `publish_at`, replace-plan ids, superseded runs' receipts), matches Phase D's exact LF title first (then the bare, dated, part-suffixed and pre-render-label forms), ignores public videos that predate the drive's first publish attempt and titles another drive derives too, and after a failed publish needs a catalog fetched since (the backlog tick refreshes it, else the next tick retries). The replace flow's LF-done resolve no longer closes an orphan row, and rows of LFs the checkpoint dropped are closed.
