### Fixed

- A drive whose bracket route names only a country or a state (a county-level or two-state label is unchanged) now titles each long-form with that label plus its own measured recording date, for example `Ireland (Sep 16, 2025)`, instead of the bare label every other same-country drive shares. The burned-in on-screen text stays the bare label, a drive with no measured date stays undated, and the exact-duplicate title refusal still runs afterwards. Phase BC now previews, hashes into the OST audit and checks that same dated title, so the title an operator confirms is the title that ships; a confirmation stored before this change still covers an unchanged burn (the added drive date alone never reopens it). A split full_drive day is dated too: Phase BC now confirms the `— Part k of K` title Phase D ships (`Ireland (Sep 16, 2025) — Part 1 of 2`, one shared `ensure_unique_titles` call), where Phase D used to ship the bare label. A title-only suffix change (the added drive date, or a split day's `(Part k of K)` becoming `— Part k of K`) no longer restales a confirmed day: the audit hash covers the title without those suffixes plus the LF's part numbers, and every hash an earlier build stored for the same burn text and base label still covers it, at `lf_gate_or_raise`, the per-day gate and the resume drift check alike (one shared rule, including a split day confirmed under its `(Part k of K)` titles, which the pass re-stamps and rewrites in the sidecar so the next resume agrees); a changed burn text or base label still reopens the day, and a stale day's refusal says the burn text changed instead of telling an unattended run to pass the flag. `--ost-audit-confirmed` and `--fresh` are one-shot and are stripped from the resume recipe by the option they resolve to in the drive parser (any abbreviation or `name=value` form too), so a heal relaunch cannot auto-confirm a changed day. Two split days sharing one label are dated on the base label and suffixed once; with no measured date that makes them unique they are refused instead of getting a second part suffix. A split LF whose title exactly matches a live video is now dated before its part suffix, or refused, like an unsplit LF, instead of being bumped into `... (Part 2) — Part 1 of 2`.
