### Fixed

- A drive whose bracket route names only a country or a state (a county-level or two-state label is unchanged) now titles each long-form with that label plus its own measured recording date, for example `Ireland (Sep 16, 2025)`, instead of the bare label every other same-country drive shares. The burned-in on-screen text stays the bare label, a drive with no measured date stays undated, and the exact-duplicate title refusal still runs afterwards. Phase BC now previews, hashes into the OST audit and checks that same dated title, so the title an operator confirms is the title that ships; a confirmation stored before this change still covers an unchanged burn (the added drive date alone never reopens it). A split full_drive day is dated too: Phase BC now confirms the `— Part k of K` title Phase D ships (`Ireland (Sep 16, 2025) — Part 1 of 2`, one shared `ensure_unique_titles` call), where Phase D used to ship the bare label. A title-only suffix change (the added drive date, or a split day's `(Part k of K)` becoming `— Part k of K`) no longer restales a confirmed day: the audit hash covers the title without those suffixes plus the LF's part numbers, and a hash an earlier build stored still covers it in exactly four forms (the normalised hash, the raw hash, the raw hash of a split day under its deployed `(Part k of K)` titles, and the raw hash of a bracket-dated day under its deployed base titles, `<label> (Part k of N)` with k and N counted across the whole drive in LF order as deployed code deduped them, so a multi-day country or state drive confirmed before dating stays confirmed on every day), each only while the burn text, base label and part count are unchanged, at `lf_gate_or_raise`, the per-day gate and the resume drift check alike (one shared rule, including a split day confirmed under its `(Part k of K)` titles, which the pass re-stamps and rewrites in the sidecar so the next resume agrees); a changed burn text, base label or part count (N, as counted over the drive) still reopens the day, and a confirm passed on this run covers only the payload this run showed (once this run's front-load has stamped a day, a per-day payload that hashes to something else is refused and names both hashes, never silently re-stamped), and a stale day's refusal says its OST audit (burn text or title) changed instead of telling an unattended run to pass the flag. `--ost-audit-confirmed` and `--fresh` are one-shot and are stripped from the resume recipe by the option they resolve to in the drive parser (any abbreviation or `name=value` form too), so a heal relaunch cannot auto-confirm a changed day. Two split days sharing one label are dated on the base label and suffixed once; with no measured date that makes them unique they are refused instead of getting a second part suffix. A split LF whose title exactly matches a live video is now dated before its part suffix, or refused, like an unsplit LF, instead of being bumped into `... (Part 2) — Part 1 of 2`. A pre-render-decision LF is now checked for uniqueness as the label Phase D ships (its decision's `input_label`, part suffixes left to be re-derived) instead of the bare burned text it was refused on and then discarded; a real duplicate of that shipped label still refuses. Tests now pin each of Phase BC's own title sites (pre-seed, demoted preview, post-render, Waypost approval) on a split day to the titles Phase D ships.
