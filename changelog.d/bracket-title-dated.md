### Fixed

- A drive whose bracket route names only a country or a state (a county-level or two-state label is unchanged) now titles each long-form with that label plus its own measured recording date, for example `Ireland (Sep 16, 2025)`, instead of the bare label every other same-country drive shares. The burned-in on-screen text stays the bare label, a drive with no measured date stays undated, and the exact-duplicate title refusal still runs afterwards.
