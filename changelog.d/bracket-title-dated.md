### Fixed

- A drive whose bracket route names only a country or a state (a county-level or two-state label is unchanged) now titles each long-form with that label plus its own measured recording date, for example `Ireland (Sep 16, 2025)`, instead of the bare label every other same-country drive shares. The burned-in on-screen text stays the bare label, a drive with no measured date stays undated, and the exact-duplicate title refusal still runs afterwards. Phase BC now previews, hashes into the OST audit and checks that same dated title, so the title an operator confirms is the title that ships; an OST audit confirmed before this change for such a drive asks for re-confirmation.
